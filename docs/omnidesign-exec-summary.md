# OmniDesign — Executive Summary

**OmniDesign generates a complete, accessible design system — light *and* dark — in minutes,
that designers, developers, and AI adapt by changing a single attribute instead of recoding
colors for every background, surface, and mode. It automatically eliminates the accessibility
failures teams most often ship — low-contrast text, components and their varying states,
small target areas, and focus-visible indicators.**

### Why it matters — risk *and* opportunity

- **~95% of the top 1M sites fail WCAG** (~51 errors/page). The **#1 failure is low-contrast
  text** — and OmniDesign designs away that plus **components and their varying states, small
  target areas, and focus-visible indicators**, in light and dark mode.
- **4,000+ ADA digital lawsuits/year**; retrofitting accessibility costs **~10×** building it in.
- **1 in 6 people (1.3B)** live with a disability, with **$200B+ US discretionary spend** —
  a market inaccessible products quietly turn away.

### The AI-cost problem it solves

- Agentic AI burns **~1,000× the tokens** of a chat (re-reading context every step);
  programming is now **>50% of all LLM tokens**; and **more tokens ≠ results** (bugs +54%,
  code churn +861% in high-AI teams).
- Teams hit **token, context, and rate limits fast — that blocks production.** OmniDesign
  cuts the styling/theme code AI must generate *and re-read* by **~90%**, and ships
  **deterministic, correct-first-time** output that doesn't churn.

### What makes it different

- **Dynamic** — one `data-theme`/`data-surface` attribute flips mode, background, or
  elevation; no variables to rewire, no component code to touch.
- **Accessible by default & validated** — contrast, focus indicators, and 24×24 target
  sizes guaranteed in both modes, with a built-in WCAG 2.2 AA checklist as proof.
- **Designer-in-the-loop** — you shape the final look and feel; OmniDesign supplies the logic.
- **Human-Intelligence, not AI** — deterministic, auditable, and a negligible energy/water
  footprint (and it cuts the tokens AI tools spend downstream).
- **Pixel-perfect design-to-code & code-to-design** — a logic-based, zero-token bridge, plus
  project files that teach AI to build correctly with the system (AI → Figma or code).
- **Beautiful** — LCH color-theory themes and accessible gradients.
- **vs. Storybook:** checks accessibility *in context*, not in isolation.
  **vs. Style Dictionary:** tokens are dynamic and accessible, not static.
